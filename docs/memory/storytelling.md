# Storytelling

Read before touching `src/tools/storytelling/` or `src/shared/story/` — the narrated series, its charte, its blocks, the private script, a post's continuity.

## Why it is a tool here, and what it is (2026-10-30)

**Decision, the maintainer's.** The series of short narrated videos he publishes (first season *Le tri d'Australie*, nine posts) was built as a Remotion project inside his `second-brain` notes; he chose to make it an Atelier tool rather than a repository of its own, named **Storytelling** («studio» already names his sketchbook and Atelier's Studio). **Why**: the posts need what Atelier already has — Winnow, the maps and drone tracks, the vehicles, the house style, the in-browser video export, the agent bridge — and a new repository would have meant wiring access and the register again. **How to apply**: the deployed tool plays and exports the posts that exist; making a new post or block is code, run locally with `npm run dev` (a short loop: the stage reloads on save), then pushed. The brief, the facts and the season's scripts live in `second-brain` (`create/social/storytelling/`), never here.

## A post is code; the series shares a charte (2026-10-30)

**Decision, his** (`second-brain`'s fiche, « Le commun et le post »): there is no universal engine that can make any post. Each post is its own composition — a skeleton in `posts.ts` today, free to grow blocks of its own — and starts as a COPY of the post before it, never from a blank template. What is shared, and is the point of the tool: the charte (`shared/story/charte.ts` — palette « lin », Geist, the « pose » tempo, captions « leve », the HUD after the hook, grain and vignette, all chosen in his labs), the blocks (`shared/story/blocks/`, `scenes/`), the voice and music, and the CONTINUITY (the order of `POSTS` is what the ending follows: it names the next post and shows its cover, `recap` + `paintCover`). **How to apply**: a new look or motion setting goes in `charte.ts`, never as a literal in a block; a block is canvas drawing at its own local clock (`(c, lt, params, ep)`) and never knows where it sits in the post; `compose.ts` places blocks on the post's clock and cross-fades them (« noir »: A out over the first half of the 0.9 s overlap, B in over the second, `slotAlpha`, pure).

## Atelier is public: the words are a private SCRIPT (2026-10-30)

**Decision, his.** The repository is public, the posts' content is not until published. A post is two halves: the SKELETON here (which blocks, how long, when a caption speaks) and the SCRIPT (`StoryScript`, a `.story.json` of `kind: 'atelier.story-script'`, version 1: the series' name, the handle, the closing request, the trip's stops, and per post its title, cover line and each block's params and caption), kept by him in `second-brain` and loaded in the tool. `assemble` (pure) joins them; without a script every post still plays with placeholder captions and an unnamed route, so a shape can be judged and nothing personal ships. **How to apply**: never write a caption, a number of his, a place, a device or a date into `posts.ts` or a block's default — a block's DEFAULTS must be neutral (the map's placeholder stops, `CARD`, the first and last stop); block params that are content belong to the script. Video text is in French (the series' language); the tool's own UI is English like the rest of the suite.

**Kept on the device**: the loaded script, in `localStorage` (`atelier.storytelling.script`, `script-store.ts`), until *Forget* — declared in the legal notice and `local-first.md`. The voice and music files are decoded into memory for the visit only.

## The gallery is the MAP OF SUBJECTS; the archive is a Winnow (2026-10-30)

**Decision, his** («gallery pour story telling en mode carte des sujets»; «winnow doit aussi marcher en local … exporter et ensuite push pour archive»). `#/storytelling` is the map (`StoryGallery.tsx` over `shared/story/story-map.ts`, pure): the script's `topics` (pillar → subject → version, a line naming a `post` IS that post) against its `channels`, each crossing a `CellState` (`idee` · `encours` · `pret` · `publie` · `non`, the Carte des sujets artifact's vocabulary, https://claude.ai/artifact/N8HGFWwiY2XT3RrNtcBTFj, which seeded his script). A click cycles a state (`nextState`, published dated today) and writes the SCRIPT (`withCell`), kept in `localStorage` and downloaded back by *Save script* — the map is content, so it lives in his private file like the words, never in this repo. A post no line names is never hidden: it shows under «Not filed». `#/storytelling/<post>` is the editor (`PostEditor.tsx`); the shell holds the script and each post's sound so a round trip keeps both. **Archive**: after an export the MP4 and its cover stay in memory and go through the one `SendFinalsPanel`, as an ARCHIVE (`finalsPlan`'s `archive.folder`: no lineage note), to any connected Winnow — the local dev stack (Winnow's `docker-compose.dev.yml` allows `http://localhost:5173`) or his server's through an app token (`architecture.md`). **Measured on a real local Winnow (2026-10-31)**: the upload is imported into Incoming and refiled by Winnow's OWN template (`incoming/unknown/<year>/<date>/`, the device unknown) — the `Storytelling/<series>/<post>` path only shapes the staging batch, so do not promise it as a place; a folder of its own would be Winnow's import template to change (its E21). **How to apply**: a new map column or state is a script field, never a new store; the archive never reaches a Winnow the person did not connect.

## Rendering: the painted export, sound from the bed (2026-10-30)

The Remotion engine already drew every block on a canvas at a time `t`, so the port is the drawing code moved as is, Remotion's `Sequence`/`AbsoluteFill` replaced by `compose.ts` (each slot painted into a layer, composited at its alpha; HUD and texture drawn on the canvas, the grain as an `overlay` at 0.055). Export is `encodeFrames` (1080 × 1920, 30 fps) with the voice and the music as `clips` of a `renderBed` (`media-pipeline.md`, «Recorded sound joins the same bed»), so they ride the same AAC lead as the ticks. **Effects are the author's files first (2026-10-30, his ask: «the audio effect pick input so I can use my own sound instead of the basic webaudio effect»).** An effect is a moment of the post with a SOURCE: a file of his (the default when one is added), or one of the synthesised `voices.ts` as a fallback. Files become `clips`, built-in voices a score, both in the one bed. Voice, music and effects are held PER POST for the visit (`sounds[postId]`), never across posts and never stored. **How to apply**: any new sound feature offers his own file before a synthesised one; Trips' opener ticks still play the built-in voices only — giving them the same file choice is the next step if he asks.

**Measured end to end on 2026-10-31, in the cloud container, on one machine**: a real Winnow (Postgres 16 + Redis + `next dev` + its worker, no Docker) indexed sample media; Atelier's dev server on `localhost:5173` connected it by cookie (same site, no token), read assets and a proxy through the client, exported EP.00 for real (H.264 1080×1920, 23.2 s, 696 frames in 18 s — this Chromium DOES encode `avc1.640028`/`4d0028` from a `localhost` page; WebCodecs is undefined on a `data:` page, which is what made earlier probes say «no encoder») and sent the MP4 and its cover, which Winnow imported (two new assets). **AAC was the one gap**: this Chromium refuses `mp4a.40.2`, so the first export went out silent with its sentence. Fixed the same day by the Opus fallback (`media-pipeline.md`, «Opus where AAC is refused»): EP.00 exported again from the UI with a voice, a music bed, one effect from a file and one built-in came out h264 + opus, 23.2 s both, «Exported, with its sound.», no page error. Still unheard by a person: the mix's balance, on his Mac.

**His workflow is Winnow REMOTE (the Optiplex) + Atelier LOCAL (2026-10-31, his correction: «winnow remote et atelier local»)**, not both on one machine — so the path that matters is the app token, not the cookie. **Measured the same day across two sites**: Winnow on `http://localhost:3000` with `CORS_ALLOWED_ORIGINS=http://127.0.0.1:5175`, Atelier's dev server on `http://127.0.0.1:5175` (`--host 127.0.0.1`; loopback, so still a secure context for WebCodecs), a fresh browser with no Winnow cookie, an editor app token: the form asked for the token, refused a bad one in words, kept the good one; `/api/auth/me` said `via token`, the list, a proxy and a thumbnail (`access_token` in the query, drawn into a canvas that stays readable) came back; EP.00 exported with its sound; the archive reached Winnow's Incoming. The emulation is that way round because `normalizeBaseUrl` accepts `http:` for `localhost` alone. **The run found one bug**: after the uploads `SendFinalsPanel` called `POST /api/reconcile`, an ADMIN route, and a token is never more than an editor — every archive by token said «2 sent, then: This account is not allowed to do that». An archive no longer reconciles (`FinalsPlan.reconcile`, nothing to link), and a refused reconcile after finals leaves them sent and says the link needs an admin. **For the Optiplex**: its stack must list `http://localhost:5173` in `CORS_ALLOWED_ORIGINS` (beside Atelier's own origin) and he mints an editor token on Users › App tokens; Cloudflare's 100 MB body limit is already advertised as `limits.maxUploadBytes` and refused before a byte moves.

## Sources: a trip and a Winnow, then frozen facts (2026-10-31)

**Decision, his, from the lab https://claude.ai/artifact/JDfkp4efvrHhFqfFFmnX8F** («ok pour tes trois recommandations»): an episode takes its data the way Flight Atlas takes flights — a trip of Trips on this device for the map (its `road`, already imported from Polarsteps with its modes and detail, and its named places; EP.00 starts in SYDNEY, not at the script's typed stops), and a connected Winnow for the numbers and the wall's pictures. **His three picks**: the wall's number is the media of the TRIP'S days (not the whole instance); the tiles are his real thumbnails sampled across every day, kept on the device; the map names the places where a stage starts or ends, and he ticks others. **The model** (`StoryScript.sources` / `facts`, a post's own over the series' — `shared/story/sources.ts`, pure): sources are names only (a trip id + its name, a Winnow source id), never the trip or the pictures, which stay in the browser; FACTS are numbers frozen with their date (`at`) and dotted keys a caption will cite (`media.total`), so a post exports the same tomorrow; changing a source drops the facts it counted. The Sources section writes the SERIES' sources (one trip per season); a per-post override is read but has no control yet. **C2 built the same day**: `trip-map.ts` (pure) reads the trip's road through `roadPieces` in the trip's OWN mode (`crow` = no road), simplified to `MAP_DETAIL_M` (1 km, or the trip's detail when coarser — a pixel is ~2 km at ×1, the camera zooms ×2.3), the pieces cut at flights joined in one line; the stops are the stage ends in date order, a town that ends one stage and starts the next named once, a road with no named ends gets two unnamed dots, never the placeholder's. `geo.setMap(stops, road)` separates the ROUTE (the road, thousands of points) from the named STOPS (`STOP_XY`, `STOP_REACH` — each reached where the road passes closest, searched forward); `mapLabels` reads those, `setStops` is `setMap` without a road. The editor reads the trip through `use-trip-map.ts` (store and road maths imported only when a trip is named); a named trip missing from this browser falls back to the script's stops. Not built: the places he ticks beyond the stage ends. **C3 built the same day**: *Count* (Sources section) asks the series' Winnow for two `calendar`s over the trip's `startDate…endDate` — every media, then `mediaType: 'photo'` — and freezes `media.total`, `media.photo`, `media.video` (the difference), `days` (days holding media) and `trip.days` (the span, both ends) into `script.facts` with `at` and `from` (`story-facts.ts`, pure: `factsFromCalendars`); *Count again* replaces them and lists what changed (`factChanges`). `assemble` fills the post's facts (its own, else the series') into the title, the cover line, every caption and every block param: `{key}` inside a text is written the French way (`factText`, `fr-FR` grouping), a param that is EXACTLY one `{key}` becomes the NUMBER (the counter runs to it; the wall formats a number itself); an uncounted key stays as written, braces shown, never 0. **Measured against a real Winnow** (the container's, by app token, cross-site): 7 assets on one day → `media.total` 5 (pairs collapsed), photo 2, video 3, `days` 1; EP.00's wall and caption read « 5 ». **How to apply**: a new number is a new key computed in `story-facts.ts` from a source, never typed in a block; the script he keeps in second-brain must say `{media.total}` where it now says « 100 000 ». **C4 built the same day**: Count also freezes `facts.tiles` — each day's `cover_id` from the same calendar, in day order, days with media only, thinned evenly to the wall's 180 tiles (`wallPicks`, pure) — ids only in the script. `wall-cache.ts` keeps a 192 px centred JPEG square of each in IndexedDB `atelier-storytelling` / `tiles`, keyed `source:id`, fetched six at a time through the client's `thumbUrl` (the token rides in the query on that route) and read back on any later visit, so the wall draws and exports with the instance unreachable; *Forget* clears the store. `Hook.ts`'s `setWall` (set by `compose.ts` from `ComposeOptions.wall`) puts picture k on tile k mod n; a tile with no picture keeps its colour. **Measured**: nine days on the container's Winnow → nine pictures, the wall and its « 13 » drawn from them, and after a reload with every thumbnail request refused the wall still had all nine. A FIFTH case of media bytes kept on the device (`local-first.md`, « Persistence »), his pick. **C5 built the same day**, on what the sources give without reading every media: Count also asks `facets()` for the library's devices and one `calendar` per device (the busiest twelve, `MAX_DEVICES`) over the span → `gear.<name squeezed to [a-z0-9]>` (`deviceKey`, names that squeeze alike are summed); `geoDays(span)` → `geo.days.located` / `geo.days.unlocated` and `facts.holes`, runs of days with media but no position (`geoFacts`; a day with no media between two unplaced ones does not close a hole); the trip itself → `trip.km` (its `tripRoadLine` km), `trip.stages`, `trip.places` (`tripFacts`). The two Winnow extras are optional — an older instance keeps the totals. `gapsOf` places a hole on the drawn route by the road's fix TIMES (`TripMap.times`), else linearly between the trip's first and last day; the editor gives them to every `map-holes` block whose params leave `gaps` unset (`withGaps`). `assemble` fills a `season-recap` without `episodes` from the season's posts (number, title, cover line). **The script wins**: his season-1 script still types `gaps` for EP.04 and `episodes` for the finale, as it typed « 100 000 » — he removes them to read the sources. **Not built**: bursts (Winnow does not count piles without reading every media); `names`' wrong/right pairs are his words, not a source. **Measured** on the container's Winnow with a test photo carrying a camera and a position: `gear.sonyilce7cm2` 1, nine unplaced days in two holes drawn dotted on EP.04's road from Sydney, EP.00's caption citing `{trip.km}`. **His asks, D1–D2 built (2026-10-31)**: `ScriptSources.trip.stages` keeps stage ids (absent = all) — the road is clipped to the kept stages' days (a fix counts when its time falls in one), the towns and `trip.*` facts read only them, and Count's span becomes the kept stages' first to last day (`countedSpan`); `ScriptSources.map` = `points` (`ends` default, `starts`, `places`) and `label` (`place`, `stage` — its name, else « first → last », `place-date`, `none`) → `tripStops` / `MapChoice`. Entries `type` (letters at `cps`, a caret) / `rise` / `word` / `fade`; a sticker line pops on a sober spring; exits `fade` / `erase` / `fly` / `cut`. ## The series' language (2026-10-31)

**Decision, his** («je vais le mettre en anglais», to reach more people than a French series would): `StoryScript.lang` (`'en' | 'fr'`, absent = French) is ONE switch for the series, under Script. `shared/story/locale.ts` holds it as module state like `geo.ts`'s map and `Hook.ts`'s wall (`setLang`, set by `compose.ts` from `Episode.lang` and by `cover.ts`): `numberText` (`en-US` / `fr-FR` grouping), `dayText` (« Sep 3 » / « 03 sept. ») and `word()`, the table of the words a BLOCK writes itself (NEXT EPISODE, SEASON, BEFORE/AFTER, MIDNIGHT, the clocks' dates, « copied », « camera », « days without a position », « photos »). `assemble` passes `lang` to `fillText` / `fillParams`, so `{media.total}` reads « 98,412 » in English. **Not translated, on purpose**: his words (titles, captions, hook, voice) — the script is written in one language, and a bilingual series would be two scripts, not a switch; the tool's own UI stays English. **How to apply**: a block that draws a fixed word or a formatted number goes through `locale.ts`, never a literal French string; a new word is a line in BOTH languages of `WORDS`.

## The series' look: the wall's grid, grain and vignette (2026-11-01)

**Decision, his ask** (« choisir le gap, la taille des photos, des photos plus grandes que d'autres, le nombre de colonnes, de rangées, la façon dont elles sont regroupées », and the vignette): `StoryScript.look` = `wall` (`WallGrid`: `cols`, `rows` 0 = square cells, `gap`, `radius`, `big` the share of the wall in 2 × 2 pictures, `order` rows / columns / centre / shuffle, `zoom` the camera's end), `vignette`, `grain` — ONE look for the series, handed to every episode by `assemble`, set as module state by `compose.ts` and `cover.ts` (`setWallGrid`, `setTexture`) like the map and the language. **Why series-level, not a block's params**: counting thins the days' pictures to the grid's cell count (`wallCells`, capped at `MAX_TILES` 400), so the grid must be known where Count runs; a grid changed after counting still draws (`pictureFor` spreads n pictures over the places in day order, repeating or skipping), and Count again gives each place its own. **`wall-grid.ts` (pure)**: the grid is laid out from its CENTRE cell, centred on the frame (the camera's first picture, the hook's photo), spilling past the edges; the default reproduces the first wall exactly (11 × 19 cells of 108 px from (−54, −66)) — the old `COLS`/`HOOK_CI` constants are gone. Big pictures are placed by a seeded draw (never over the centre cell), so the same settings always give the same wall; the layout is memoised by its key. The camera starts with the centre picture 648 px wide (×6 on 108 px) and ends at `zoom`. Pictures are cropped from their centre to a place's shape (the cache keeps 192 px squares). **Not built**: grouping by stage (the tiles are ids in day order, with no stage attached); a per-post look.

**Not yet built**: commands for an agent (`storytelling.open`, a snapshot at a time — `agent-commands.md`); the script kept on a Winnow like a trip (today a file he saves back); photographs from the Library or Winnow in the blocks (they still draw colour tiles); a post that is more than a skeleton (its own block file).
