## One recap, several networks (2026-11-11, lab 18)

His yes to every recommendation of lab 18 (https://claude.ai/artifact/N9KDnHE6SE8d5c3JPUeRMn — published as «lab 17» the day another session's lab 17, the cells', was merged; called 18 here). He had added TikTok, YouTube, Bluesky, Threads and X to his Postiz and found two walls with Instagram's music there: a publication failing on a bitrate limit, and no way to hear a track or choose its moment.

**The networks are a pure table** (`postiz/networks.ts`, read from each channel's schema on his Postiz that day): caption length (2 200 · 2 000 · 5 000 · 300 · 500 · 280), a title (TikTok 90, YouTube 100 and REQUIRED), what a post holds (`networkPlan`: the film everywhere, a story on Instagram only, a carousel cut to the network's count — the first pictures where clips cannot mix, his Q6), the provider settings each schema requires (`settingsFor`: TikTok's nine required fields with `DIRECT_POST` — `UPLOAD` only drops the file in the app's inbox —, YouTube's title/type/kids/tags, X's `who_can_reply_post`, Bluesky and Threads their `__type` alone). A provider outside the six is listed on Sources greyed («Recap does not post to … yet»), never guessed at. `postBody` builds every post through `settingsFor`; a media's kind is read from its path (`.mp4`).
- **Sources** lists every channel with its network's mark (`NetworkMark.tsx`: the network's own fixed colour as inline rgb — data like a flag, and the lint's raw-hex rule is about class names); the ringed one (`channelId`) is only the one CHECKED FIRST (`defaultDestinations`); *Refresh* re-asks `/integrations` after he adds a network in Postiz.
- **TikTok trap, not yet met on his account**: an app TikTok has not audited may post to private accounts only — Postiz answers `unaudited_client_can_only_post_to_private_accounts`; `failureHint` says it. Recap sends `PUBLIC_TO_EVERYONE`; a visibility choice is his to ask for.

**One render, one upload per file, one post per network** (`recap-post.ts`, `sendPosts`, spec'd on a fake client): `networkPlan` says per destination which files it takes, the union is uploaded once (a Postiz media goes on any number of posts), then `POST /posts` is called ONCE PER CHANNEL — never one body with several `posts` entries, which Postiz would refuse whole for one network's bad settings. A network that cannot take the kind, whose file is too heavy, or that Postiz refuses lands in `failed` with its reason and `failureHint`; the others go (his recommendation «un échec ne bloque pas les autres»). All refused before upload → it throws, nothing leaves. `Publication` gained `network` (the provider) and `group` (one send; null and `instagram` for what lab 12 kept); `publicationGroups` is what *Sent* and the gallery read. The Instagram-only options (Trial Reel, collaborator) are sent on Instagram's post alone.
- **Where** is a chip per account: its mark, its name, what it RECEIVES (`plan.as`) or two words of why not (`plan.short`), disabled when refused. The ticks are the last send's for this TEMPLATE on this device (`publish-where.ts`, `localStorage` `atelier.recap.publish.where.v1`; his Q5), else the one ringed on Sources.
- **The Export tab follows the foot's switch** (built beyond the lab, to say when he asks): under *Save* it shows Export (which files), under *Publish* Where · When · Words · Sent — seven sections at once made him scroll past what the verb would not use. Not connected, the «connect on Sources» line still shows under Save.
- **The lab drew no «Publish as»**: the Reel · Carousel · Story switch stayed, at the head of Where, since the chips' words depend on it.
- **The rhythm counts a moment once** (`postMoments`, his Q3): posts on different channels within 3 h fold into the first — the same recap on six networks, staggered or not, takes one slot and one of the week's ceiling; two posts on ONE channel never fold. *Instagram first, the others 2 h later* is `at + 2 h` for every non-Instagram destination (a draft stays a draft).
- **For an agent**: `recap.schedule` takes `on` (`pickChannels`, pure: `["all"]`, a network, an account's name or a unique part of it; nothing = where the template went last) and `stagger`, and answers `posts` (one id per network), `failed`, `notes`; `recap.calendar` lists `accounts`.

**Words per network** (`recap-publish.ts`, `PublishWords.tsx`; his Q2: «raccourcies automatiquement puis relues»). `RecapPublish.captions` / `.titles` hold a network's OWN template and title BY CHANNEL ID (absent = the shared caption); `networkCaption` fills either and fits it with `fitCaption` (pure, `networks.ts`: untouched when it fits; else the hashtags lifted out, the words cut at the last sentence or line end past 40 % of the room — else at a word with «…» —, then the first two hashtags back on a line of their own; X counts an astral character twice). `networkTitle`: his own, else the heading, cut at a word. Any change to a network's words or title takes the approval back like the caption's; ONE approval covers every network, since a fitted text is derived from the approved one.
- **The dot** (a tab whose words Atelier shortened and he has not opened) is the session's (`seen`, by channel → the text he saw): a nudge and a *Words · 1 to read* on the band, never a gate — an agent's send needs the approval only.
- Typing in a network's tab makes those words its own (the shown cut becomes the template); *The shared words* drops them. A duplicate and a Suggestion's `readyRecap` carry `captions` and `titles` with the caption (same accounts on the same device).
- **For an agent**: `recap.postCaption` takes `on` (one account or network) and `title`; it answers `accounts` — each network's length, whether its words are its own or were shortened, and the text when it differs from the caption.
