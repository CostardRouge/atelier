# Recap

Read before touching `src/shared/recap/` or `src/tools/recap/` — the instrument that turns captures into social recaps (the month calendar, on this day, a week, a grid, a single picture, a photo dump). Brief: `docs/recap.md`; canvas lab 1: https://claude.ai/artifact/9jwFzpczK7jSA6QteE4KzM.

## A template is a recipe over five axes (2026-11-04, R1)

**Decision.** `RecapDoc` (`recap-doc.ts`) holds a `template` and every axis as its own field: the selection rule (`anchor`, `rungs`, `range`, `filter`), the layout (`format`, `grid`, `count`), the background, what a cell says (`cells`), the motion, the sound and one `look`. `RECIPES` is the only place a template differs: it seeds the fields; nothing downstream switches on a template except `recap-layout.ts` (where the slots sit) and `recap-slots.ts` (which slots exist). **Why**: his brief — a catalogue whose every template can be re-dressed in the inspector, and a new template without a new editor. **How to apply**: a new template is a recipe, a slot list and a page layout; a new setting is a field read by `readRecapDoc` with its clamp.

## Slots, filled by the rule, corrected by hand (2026-11-04, R1)

`resolveRecap` names a template's SLOTS — a day (`2026-10-14`), a step back (`ago:1y`), a place (`n:3`) — and fills each from the pool through the filter: Winnow's pick, then its stars, then a photo over a clip (unless clips are asked), then the picture nearest 13:00. `picks` pins a picture on a slot and wins; `cleared` empties one; a picture pinned elsewhere is never the rule's choice again (the same picture twice reads as a mistake). Grid/single/dump spread `spreadPick` over the period: ranked pictures first, the rest evenly spaced in time. Days are capture-wall-clock `YYYY-MM-DD` (`captureDay`), never instants: a month back of 31 March is 28 February (`dayAgo`). Storytelling's wall rule, applied again.

## One layout in page pixels, one clock, one painter (2026-11-04, R1)

`layoutRecap` places every part in the page's own pixels (1080 wide, the format's height; Reel insets keep 12 % top, 16 % bottom free for Instagram's buttons); `planFilm` times every entrance, clip turn and the film's length; `paintRecap` draws a page at a moment through `PaintSources` (sync — a picture not in hand is a placeholder, never a wait). The stage and every export call the same three, so the preview IS the file (Mosaic's and Storytelling's rule). `hitTest` reads the same boxes for a click. A dump's pages follow `dumpPages`: a cover, then oneTwo · full · four · two · three, four or fewer left ending on ONE fitted page, never past 20 slides. Only the cells WITH a picture enter (`planFilm`'s `filled`, `filledOf(resolved)`): an empty day is drawn with the grid — it fades in with the weekdays, never with the wave — and the wave runs through the pictures with no gap where a day is empty (his report, 2026-11-05). The shadow under the pictures is `cells.shadow/shadowBlur/shadowY` (darkness 0 = none), set by the recipe (month, grid, dump) and in Layout › Shadow. Clips play `turn` (one at a time after everything is on, ≤ 5 s each, ringed in the accent) by default; `clipSlideNumbers` numbers the carousel slide each clip gets after the recap's own pages — the cell's `▶ 4`. A sound marked `fit` stretches the film to a whole number of its loops, unless that would more than double it. The author's LENGTH (`doc.length`, Export › Length: Auto · 7 · 15 · 30 · Other, 3–90 s) wins over that fit (his rule, 2026-11-05): longer re-plans with a longer hold per page, the rest on the last page; shorter SCALES the whole plan (entrances, turns, pages alike) — a clip still plays at its own speed inside its shortened turn; the sound is cut to it with the film's fade. Checked in node through Storytelling's recording context (`recap-paint.test.ts`).

## A picture is KEPT as a print; a clip whole (2026-11-04, R2)

`atelier-recap` keeps, per picture KEY (shared by every recap of the device), the PRINT a slot draws — a JPEG ≤ 2048 px: a Winnow photo cut from its proxy, a Winnow clip's poster from its thumbnail, a local photo through `decodePhoto` (HEIF, JXL, a RAW's render), a local clip's poster from a frame at 0.6 s — and the CLIPS a recap plays whole (Winnow's proxy or the local file). **Why**: a local `File` does not survive a reload (`architecture.md`), and a recap must reopen and export without its files; a print is made only for a picture a slot SHOWS, never for the pool. Pruned to what the device's recaps name when one is deleted; said in the legal notice and `local-first.md` (seventh case). The sound file is kept per recap (`sounds`), never in the document. A clip added as the looping BACKGROUND joins the pool `loose` (`RecapPicture.loose`): kept and synced like any picture, never a slot's — found driving the carousel, where the background clip had taken the cell of the day it was shot.

## The rule fills once; the stage drives the inspector (2026-11-04, R2)

A new recap with a Winnow connected FILLS ITSELF once (`recap-fill.ts`: one `allAssets` window per run of days, rejects left out, references only). A click on the stage selects a cell, a text or the background and the inspector jumps to its tab (Pictures, Text, Background); a drag swaps two cells' pictures (both pinned after) or moves the heading (`headingOffset`). Paused, the stage RESTS at the page's `allOn` (everything on, an edit seen at once); Play starts the film at 0 unless the author scrubbed. The catalogue's cards are the real painter over colour fields (`sample-recap.ts` — no photograph in a public repo) and play their entrance on hover.

## Develop per picture, one look per recap (2026-11-04, R2)

Develop's own sheet opens on the print (or the local file in hand): Done writes the picture's `develop` and the recap's `look` (the stack's layers). The stage and the exports grade each print through `makeFrameGrader(stack.composeWith(develop))` once per look + develop (`use-recap-pictures.ts`), the Trips/Studio rule that what was done to a photograph is part of it. Clips are drawn ungraded in V1.

## Exports paint on their own clip elements (2026-11-04, R2)

`recap-export.ts`: the film is `encodeFrames` over the painter, every clip and the looping background SEEKED to the exact frame before it is drawn (the stage's elements are left alone, Mosaic's rule); the sound is the kept file looped by `renderBed` clips with the AAC lead, the FILM's fades baked after (not each loop's). Stills: each page at its `allOn`. Carousel: the pages, then one MP4 per clip (≤ 30 s, cover-fit), named by the slide numbers the cells' badges print. A recap kept on a Winnow needs its bucket to list the `recap` kind — his change in Winnow, as `mosaic` was.

## Every text has its own style; presets are the suite's title styles (2026-11-05)

His review: the face was global. `RecapDoc.texts` holds a `TextStyle` per ROLE (`recap-text.ts`: heading, subheading, cta, weekdays, dayNumber, cellWords, labels — `rolesOf(template)` lists a template's): face (Storytelling's `STORY_FONTS`, or `auto` = the face the place draws by default under `doc.font`), size (×, the layout sizes heading/sub/cta/weekdays boxes with it), weight (snapped to what the face ships), colour (null = ink by ground), case and spacing (null = the place's own), an OUTLINE (stroked under the fill at twice its width), a SHADOW under the letters and a GLOW (the title styles' bleed + halo, `glowLayersFor`). A role absent draws EXACTLY as before. `TEXT_PRESETS` are `TITLE_STYLE_PRESETS` (Neutral, Or ciné, Pixel CRT, Rouge plein cadre — his «hors ciné, red») read as text styles plus Outline and Soft shadow; *Apply to every text* copies one role's style to all. The Text tab is one text at a time, the one clicked on the stage (a weekday header hits now); Storytelling's `FontPicker` tries a face on the STAGE only (`view` in the editor, never saved) and the faces in use are `document.fonts.load`ed before painting (canvas text needs them). `cells.numberAt` places a cell's number (four places).

## A frame is a focus and a zoom, and it belongs to the PICTURE (2026-11-05, framing lab)

His pick (framing lab, every default validated): never a crop rectangle. `recap-frame.ts` (pure): `Frame` = the point of the source kept in the middle (x, y as fractions) + a zoom over the cover fit (1–4); `coverWindow` is the window a box shows, clamped inside the source so no empty band ever shows; `panFrame` re-centres on what is SHOWN, so a drag past an edge stores no focus the clamp hides. A format or cell shape change keeps what was framed. `RecapPicture.frame` (it follows the picture to another cell, page, template or format) and `RecapBackground.frame`; absent = centred (`readFrame` stores nothing for the default). The one painter applies it (`drawCover(…, frame)`) — stage, poster, exports alike. Two faces edit ONE frame: A1 the Background tab's `FrameBox` (Mosaic's target frame: the whole source, the page's shape on it, drag moves, a corner zooms, a «9:16 · change in Layout ›» chip, since the format is Layout's) and a cell's *Its frame* (zoom, Centre, Frame on the stage); A2 a DOUBLE-CLICK on the stage (a cell's picture, or a blurred/video background): drag moves, the wheel or a pinch zooms, a floating bar (− % + Centre Done), Escape/Enter leaves; a cell being framed shows the whole picture faint around it (`sourceRect`, a ghost canvas over the stage — clipped at the page's edge). Framing reads its sources through `peek` (above). Agents: `recap.frame` for a slot, `recap.set` background.frame. **Rotation** (his ask, same day): `Frame.rotate` (−45…45°, absent = 0) straightens a picture; `turnedBox` gives the upright box the turned box needs, the window is fitted to THAT, so the box's corners always land on picture (the spec walks the four corners) — `framedDraw` is the painter's arithmetic, drawn turned around the box's centre and clipped by the caller (a cell's round rect, the page). A drag on a turned picture is turned back into its axes (`panFrame`). Faces: *Straighten* sliders (FrameBox, *Its frame*), ↺ ↻ by half a degree in the stage's bar, Shift + wheel; the FrameBox draws the shown region turned. ## The background clip comes from a drawer; the loop is a band (2026-11-05)

His asks: a select of names could not tell clips apart. `ClipDrawer` (a modal, full screen under 820) shows tiles — thumbnail, length, a vertical mark, playing under the pointer (the file in hand, the copy kept, else Winnow's proxy) — grouped by `backgroundChoices` (`recap-clips.ts`, pure): the recap's DAYS first (what the Winnow fill brought: clips, then Live Photos LAST with a mauve dot, `--color-live`), *Kept for this recap* (loose), the pool's others; *A file…* adds a loose clip. A Live Photo is (a) a Winnow still whose row says `group_kind: live_photo` → `RecapPicture.motion` = its `.mov`'s asset, offered as the loose clip it would become (`liveClipOf`, `liveStill` = the still, whose thumbnail it shows; the clip is fetched from its proxy, else its ORIGINAL — a companion may have no proxy; unverified against his instance), or (b) a local `.mov` of ≤ 4 s beside a still of its stem. ## A cell's words never pass the cell (2026-11-05)

His report: on a photo dump the camera line ran out of its cell. `recap-cellwords.ts` (pure): WHICH settings — `cells.exifParts` (device, lens, focal, aperture, shutter, iso; the lens off by default, which is what the line said before) and a picture's own `RecapPicture.exifParts` over it (*Its camera line*, «As every cell» off); HOW — `cells.arrange`: `line`, `stack` (one per line) or `auto` (greedy packing into lines that fit, every bit on its own line in a cell taller than wide); and every cell text drawn with a `maxWidth` (`styled` → `fitText`: shrinks to 75 %, then cuts with «…»), lines past the cell's room left out. ## An edit never moves the stage (2026-11-05, his report)

Every setting changed (a slide's layout, the background) sent the stage back to slide 1: the effect showing a selected cell's page ran on every `layout` change, and `pageOfSlot` answers the FIRST page holding the cell (a week's slide or a highlight shares day slots with the calendar).
