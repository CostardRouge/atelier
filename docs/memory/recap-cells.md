## Who a cell setting belongs to: the first slide, the family, this slide (2026-11-10, lab 17 part I)

His report: «On every cell» set the calendar and the highlights with the same switches — he could not take the day numbers off the calendar and keep them on the highlights — and a highlight's «day number» was its RANK (`flowPages` numbers its cells `numberFrom + i + 1`). **Model** (`recap-scope.ts`, pure): what a cell SAYS (`CellWords`: number, date, numberAt, count, exif, device, caption, exifParts, arrange) has three owners — the first slide (`cells`), the slides after it as a family (`carousel.cells`: flow `p:` and week `w:` pages, never `first` nor a sheet's `s:`), one slide (`carousel.slideCells[key]`, kept like `layouts`) — merged by `wordsOn`; the painter draws each page through `docOn` (a cached view whose `cells` are that page's). **A change on the first slide never moves the family** (`withWords('first')` pins, in `carousel.cells`, the old value of every word the family had not set — the first build let the calendar's «None» take the highlights' dates, the very bug he reported). A slide's value equal to its family's is dropped (its dot goes when nothing is left). **Dates** (`cellDate`): `auto` (what a cell always printed: the day on a calendar or a week, the rank elsewhere — so every older recap draws identically, Q2), `d` · `wd` · `dMon` · `wdMon` · `full` · `rank`; hidden stays `number: false`. A NEW month seeds `carousel.cells = {date: 'dMon'}` (Q1); `createRecapDoc` writes `defaultCarousel` whole because `readCarousel` gives a carousel read without words `cells: {}` (an old document). `numberAt: 'foot'` = a band low over the picture, the date on one line (refused on a calendar cell in the panel). The highlights' dates wear the text role `flowDate` («Highlight dates»), which borrows `dayNumber`'s style until set (`textStyleOf`), listed in Text only where there is a family (`rolesOf(template, hasFamily)`). `{dates}` became `carousel.headDates`: the running head says its slide's days (`daysRange`: «3 – 8 Nov», in the slide's format, `auto`/`rank` read as `dMon`) — a toggle under *Running head*, not a free word (differs from the lab). **The panel** (`CellsSection.tsx`): the chip (first scope named Calendar · Week · Cover · First slide, the family Highlights · Weeks · Pictures) follows the stage and choosing a scope shows its first slide; date tiles drawn on Monday 10 November 2025, *Where* beside them (moved out of the Text tab), *What else it says*, then the scope's own rows — the calendar's (weekdays, Sunday first, today, a day with no picture), the family's (the title on the other slides, the head's days, the order given back), the slide's (its layout, moved from the old *Slides* section, which is gone; «Back to the highlights»). On *This slide* an inherited row is greyed, a set one has ↺. *On every slide* keeps gap, corners, clip badge, clips as slides; Shadow stays its own section (the lab put a shadow switch there). No chip where there is no family (a grid without highlights, the Sheets, Wrapped, one second a day); a dump gets Cover · Pictures for free (Q5). **Differs from the lab**: the chip is not sticky (the section band already is). Agents: `recap.set` cells.date / carousel.cells / carousel.slideCells / carousel.headDates; `recap.get`'s `slideWords` (each slide's merged words). Driven headless at 1280 and 390: calendar None with highlights «10 Oct», slide 4 alone in the full date under the picture.
