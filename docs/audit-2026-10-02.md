# Audit — 2026-10-02

Phases 0 to 2 are read-only; phase 3 lists what was executed on this branch
and what waits. Every claim points at a file and a line read this session, or
at a measurement made here — headless Chromium on SwiftShader against the dev
server, a software GPU, so only COUNTS of work (contexts, draws, uploads,
readbacks) compare with a real machine, never times. Nothing in this document
was run on the maintainer's Mac or iPhone.

How to read a finding: `ID` · severity (critical / high / medium / low) ·
location · problem · impact · fix · effort (S: under an hour, one commit; M:
one commit, hours; L: several commits) · risk of fixing · verification.
*Verified* means the cited lines were read in this session; the six domain
audits were run by sub-agents and every finding kept here was re-read before
inclusion.

## Phase 1 — Findings

### Develop — stability and fidelity (the reported freeze)

**DEV-01 — the stage's WebGL2 context was thrown away and rebuilt per slider step** · Severity: critical · **FIXED on this branch (`36398f3`)**
- Location: `src/shared/develop/use-develop-picture.ts:163–232` (`graderFrom`).
- Problem: with a fresh Subject layer (sliders at zero, no look) the only reason the stage needs the GPU is a *looking* pass (the mask outline, the blink). The histogram, `delivered()` and the snapshot ask for the picture WITHOUT those passes, found `!needsGpu`, and disposed the slot's grader — the next paint rebuilt a WebGL2 context, recompiled the shaders and re-uploaded the source (a 4K bitmap, or a RAW's half-floats).
- Impact: this is the reported freeze — on a DNG every step of Tolerance, Grow, Shrink or a blink cost a full context rebuild. Measured headless: 22 contexts for a 12-step Tolerance drag before, 0 after; Grow 23 → 0; the same on a JPEG and on a DNG opened on the sensor.
- Fix (done): a slot's grader is kept while its source lives; `!needsGpu` returns the source and disposes nothing.
- Verification: the bench in `docs/memory/testing.md` («The layers bench»); `check-render.mjs` and `check-bands.mjs` green after the change.

**DEV-02 — layer thumbnails and the colour sample borrowed the stage's grader** · Severity: high · **FIXED (`1d4bb9b`)**
- Location: `use-develop-picture.ts` (`drawBelow`, now `belowSlot`), `layer-render.ts` (`LayerPassCache.cubeOf`, the `lender`).
- Problem: `layerInput`/`sampleColour` called `graderFor` on the stage slot with the layers BELOW the open one, so every pass above, the overlay and the film were released and re-uploaded on the next paint — and where nothing below needed the GPU, DEV-01 fired as well.
- Fix (done): a 512 px grader of its own that borrows the stage cache's baked cubes (a layer is baked once) and never its passes.
- Verification: `layer-prime.test.ts` (3 specs on `cubeOf`), the bench.

**DEV-03 — the subject model ran synchronously on the main thread** · Severity: high · **FIXED (`d3a5aba`)**
- Location: `src/shared/segment/segmenter.ts`, new `segment-worker.ts` + `segment-worker-client.ts`.
- Problem: MediaPipe's `InteractiveSegmenter` ran in the page; a tap froze the UI for the inference (seconds on a 4K source, on SwiftShader).
- Fix (done): a classic worker (the bundle needs `importScripts`, so not a module worker) over an `OffscreenCanvas`, the source transferred as an `ImageBitmap` once per identity; `localStorage['atelier.segment']='page'` forces the old path.
- Verification: driven headless (the panel's «tap a point to drop it» text, the mask pixels counted); not on a real GPU.

**DEV-04 — the resting pointer's readout re-read the canvas on every render** · Severity: medium · **FIXED (`5faae61`)**
- Location: `use-develop-picture.ts` (`readAfterPaint`).
- Problem: a `useEffect` with no dependency list re-armed a `getImageData` read on every render of the hook, not every paint.
- Fix (done): the read is requested once, after the paint.

**FID-01 — the stage and the export assemble the pass chain in two places** · Severity: medium · Verified
- Location: `src/shared/develop/use-develop-picture.ts:276–297` (the stage, `graderFrom`) and `src/shared/develop/roll-render.ts:218–242` (`picturePasses`, the delivery, the HDR twin and the making-of).
- Problem: the two lists are maintained by hand and agree today (gain → repair → detail-pre; geometry → layers → detail-post → vignette), but nothing holds them together: the stage adds its looking passes (clip, overlay, flash) after the vignette, the export sets `cameraWarp` from the calibration inside `picturePasses` while the stage's caller sets it on `geometry`, and `picturePasses` is imported by no spec (TEST-02 §1). `docs/memory/develop-roll.md` already names it «the seam the stage's `graderFrom` still duplicates».
- Impact: the next pass added to one list and not the other ships a preview that is not the export — the class of bug the whole «preview = export» rule exists to prevent, and no gate sees it (`check-render.mjs` compares the core to the cube, not the stage to the delivery).
- Fix: have `graderFrom` call `picturePasses` for the picture's part (with the layer-pass builder injected, so the stage keeps its `LayerPassCache`) and append its looking passes; a spec that asserts the stage's pass ids are the export's plus the looking ones.
- Effort: M · Risk of fixing: low (the order is unchanged; `check-render.mjs` covers the pixels).
- Verification: a `roll-render.test.ts` over `picturePasses`' ids for a settings record that turns every pass on; the stage's bench unchanged.
